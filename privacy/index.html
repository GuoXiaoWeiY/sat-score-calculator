Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or any personal information. The scores you calculate are processed entirely in your browser — the numbers you enter are never sent to us or anyone else.

What we collect

  • Your inputs: nothing. Your correct-answer counts are used only inside your browser to compute an estimate, and are not stored or transmitted.
  • Anonymous usage analytics: like most websites, we use Google Analytics 4 and PVUV.ai to count visits and page views. These services receive anonymized information such as your browser type, approximate region, and pages visited. We do not use advertising cookies or build user profiles.

What this site does not do

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No emails or newsletters
  • No ads or ad trackers
  • No selling or sharing of data

Third-party services

This site loads scripts from Google Analytics (Google's privacy policy) and PVUV.ai. Both are standard, privacy-respecting analytics tools. You can block them with any ad blocker or your browser's tracking protection, and the calculator will still work normally.

Children's privacy

This site does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is nothing to collect.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change too.
