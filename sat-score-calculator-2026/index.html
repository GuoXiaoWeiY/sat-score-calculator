The Digital SAT continued unchanged into 2026: two sections (Reading & Writing and Math), two adaptive modules each, and a total score from 400 to 1600. This 2026 SAT score calculator turns your practice-test correct-answer counts into a scaled estimate so you can track where you stand against your target score.

What changed for the Digital SAT in 2026?

Nothing major changed in the test format for 2026. The Digital SAT remains fully adaptive at the module level: your performance on Module 1 determines whether you receive an easier or harder Module 2. Scoring still runs from 200 to 800 per section, with no penalty for wrong answers. Because module difficulty affects the scaled score, a raw correct-answer count can only give an estimate — which is exactly what this calculator provides.

How to calculate your 2026 SAT score from correct answers

After a practice test, count the scored questions you answered correctly in each of the four modules. Enter Reading & Writing Module 1 and 2 (up to 27 each) and Math Module 1 and 2 (up to 22 each) in the tool above. We convert each section to the 200–800 scale using published practice-test score ranges and add them together. Your result is an estimate, not an official College Board score.

2026 SAT score ranges: what is competitive?

The national average stays near 1050. Scores around 1200 are competitive at many state universities, and 1400+ is strong for selective schools. Use this calculator after every practice test to see your trend across the year and focus your prep on the section that needs the most work.

Frequently asked questions

Is the SAT score calculator accurate for 2026?

It gives a close estimate based on publicly available official practice-test score ranges. Because the Digital SAT is adaptive and College Board uses equating, your official score can differ by roughly 30–60 points. Only College Board can issue an official score.

Does the 2026 Digital SAT have a guessing penalty?

No. You earn credit only for correct answers; wrong and blank answers both score zero. Always answer every question, even if you have to guess.

How many questions are on the 2026 Digital SAT?

Reading & Writing has two modules of 27 scored questions; Math has two modules of 22 scored questions. Some questions are unscored pretests, so enter only your scored correct-answer counts.
