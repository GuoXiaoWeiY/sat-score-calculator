Contact

Last updated: November 2026

There is no contact form here on purpose — a form would mean collecting your name and email, which is exactly what this site avoids. The fastest way to reach the person who maintains this calculator is through GitHub.

Open an issue (preferred): corrections, bug reports, and feature requests all go to the same place, and you can see whether someone already reported it.

Open an issue on GitHub →

A GitHub account is required to post an issue. No account? Use the profile link in the footer to find another way to get in touch.

What to include

The more specific the report, the faster it gets fixed:

  • A wrong score: the raw counts you entered (Reading & Writing modules, Math modules) and the score the calculator returned.
  • An outdated number: the page URL, the figure you believe is wrong, and a link or citation to the official source.
  • A bug: what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and your browser.
  • A broken link or a page that won't load: the URL and roughly when it happened.

What gets priority

  • Corrections to scoring data — highest priority. If a conversion or percentile figure disagrees with an official College Board source, it gets fixed first.
  • Calculator bugs — anything that produces a wrong or impossible number (for example a score outside 400–1600).
  • Accessibility problems — unusable on a screen reader, keyboard, or phone.
  • Feature requests — read and considered, but not guaranteed. The site stays deliberately small.

Response time

This is a solo-maintained project, so replies are usually within a few days rather than hours. Corrections that affect the accuracy of a published number are handled first. There is no support phone number, no chat widget, and no newsletter — just the issue tracker.

What we can't help with

  • Official scores and score reports — only the College Board can issue or change those. Log in to your College Board account.
  • Test registration, accommodations, or fee waivers — handled by the College Board or your school counselor.
  • College admissions chances — this site estimates scores, not admission odds.
